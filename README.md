# yapper

Learn Slang and Idioms in Korean, Chinese, and Spanish!
Download on the Apple [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yapper-slang-language-app/id6742934316) today!

Master the most popular slang, idioms, and expressions with an interactive and engaging app! Whether you're a language learner or just want to sound like a native, this app makes learning fun and effortless.

Features:
Swipe through flashcards with slang, pronunciation, and real-life examples
Practice speaking with audio and pronunciation guides
Track your progress and level up your language skills
